## Direction contract

THESIS: A dark glass trading desk. Frosted panels float over a graphite field lit by two soft lights, gold and blue, and the hierarchy is loud: page, section, panel title, figure, label, note. It refuses both the flat widget grid and v2's cockpit instrument.

OWN-WORLD: Graphite #0a0b0f lit by a gold glow and a blue glow. Three glass levels (panel, chrome, inset) with a light edge and a top highlight. Apple palette with fixed meanings: gold for brand and computed levels, blue for the user, green up, red down, orange caution. Geist and Geist Mono, capsule controls, 20/12 px concentric radii, Phosphor icons.

STORY: The trader sees the gold price and the session status at once, reads the chart with the levels on a TradingView-style axis, scans the plan for the day (scenarios, factor balance, their own plan) and then the risk and the calendar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: a floating glass sidebar on the left. Main column: page title "Hoy", the section "Mercado ahora", five status tiles with the gold price at 32 px first, then the 15-minute chart (8 columns, 460 px fixed, narrow native price axis with gold level labels) next to the levels list (4 columns, same height). Mobile: floating capsule tab bar at the bottom, tiles two across, then a 340 px chart.

FORM: User-pinned Liquid Glass web approximation over the existing product. No seed roll this round (the previous seed 2f0cdfc7 chose the cockpit, which this replaces at the user's request). Signature: the glass desk with the gold and blue light behind it; hovering a level row lifts its line on the chart.
